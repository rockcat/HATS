# Six Short Poems About Dogs

## 1. Faithful Friend
A wagging tail, a gentle eyes,
A heart that holds no compromise.
Through thick and thin, through joy and pain,
A loyal bond that will remain.

## 2. Morning Joy
The sun breaks through, you stretch and yawn,
Your excitement greets the dawn.
A leap, a spin, a playful bound,
Pure happiness without a sound.

## 3. The Watcher
By the window, patient still,
Ears alert upon the hill.
Watching, waiting, always true,
The world outside means less than you.

## 4. Simple Pleasures
A ball, a stick, a patch of grass,
The hours and the worries pass.
No thought of yesterday or morrow,
No envy, greed, or borrowed sorrow.

## 5. Unconditional
You ask for nothing but a touch,
A home, some care—it's not much.
Yet give so much without demand,
The finest friend in all the land.

## 6. Eternal Companion
Through every season, every year,
Your steady paws bring comfort near.
When all else fades and time moves on,
Your love remains when you are gone.
